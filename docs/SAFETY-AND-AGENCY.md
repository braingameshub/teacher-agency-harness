## What the teacher sees

For each skill the run prints the evidence, the proposed next move, the flag, the license, and the attribution. The evidence is the misconception sentence and the construct that graph says it hinders. The flag says whether the move dropped a required name or repeated the previous move.

## What the teacher can do

- Accept. The proposed move proceeds. The flag from that proposal stays visible.
- Edit. The teacher's replacement proceeds, including when the flag stays on. The harness does not lock the teacher out of a replacement.
- Stop. The move does not proceed. Nothing is substituted.

Stop is the block. A flag is information. The teacher remains the person who lets a move stand.

## What is not stored

The check skill holds accept, edit, or stop in memory for that process. It does not write a student record, a roster, a score, or a file. The sample has no student identifier. Coordinate does not run unless the teacher passes `--stalled` and the previous move. The harness does not infer that a student is stuck and does not invent a history.

This package collects no new student data. It is not a clinical instrument and it is not a research-validated assessment.

## What is withheld

- The full Learning Commons graph is not vendored. Eight CC BY 4.0 pairs are.
- Kaggle Eedi files are CC BY-NC 4.0 and are not included.
- A NonCommercial, ShareAlike, or NoDerivatives dataset fails `assert_shippable` and is not loaded.
- Standards and crosswalks, the Math Coherence Map, EssayJudge, and GSM8k are not in this repository.

Phase 3 of the proposal does not publish a final funded package until the program team has completed its safety review. This note does not claim that review has happened.
